## Slide 14

### 13 • MANAGERIAL ACTION PLAN

Actionable Recommendations for Managers
1. Mandate Early Option Transparency: Share all feasible design options
and heritage trade-offs during initial consultation.
2. Trigger Dynamic Risk Reviews: Require formal design reassessment
whenever new archaeological evidence emerges.
3. Lock Heritage into Core GIS/ERP: Prevent deletion of physical buffer
zones after statutory consent.
4. Establish Stop-Work Escalation: Grant heritage leads authority to
pause drilling before physical lock-in.
5. Audit Inﬂuence, Not Meeting Counts: Measure whether stakeholder
feedback actually modiﬁes operational plans.
