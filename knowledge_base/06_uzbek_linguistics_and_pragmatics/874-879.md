# **O‘TKIR HOSHIMOVNING  “DUNYONING ISHLARI” QISSASI  MATNINING PSIXOLINGVISTIK TAHLILI** 

# **Toshtemirova Dinora** 

_Alisher Navoiy nomidagi Toshkent davlat o‘zbek tili va adabiyoti universiteti 4-bosqich talabasi_ 

# **ANNOTATSIYA** 

_Mazkur maqolada O‘tkir Hoshimovning “Dunyoning ishlari” qissasi psixolingvistik nuqtai nazardan tahlil qilinadi. Tadqiqotda badiiy matnning kitobxon ongida qabul qilinishi, emotsional-ekspressiv birliklarning ruhiy ta’siri, ona konseptining lingvistik va psixologik xususiyatlari hamda assotsiativ tafakkur mexanizmlari o‘rganildi. Asarda qo‘llangan til vositalarining o‘quvchi idrokiga ta’siri psixolingvistika nazariyalari asosida izohlandi. Tahlillar natijasida qissaning kuchli psixologik ta’siri, avvalo, undagi emotsional birliklar, xalqona ifodalar, dialogik nutq va milliy-madaniy konseptlar bilan bog‘liqligi aniqlandi. Ona obrazi asarning konseptual markazi sifatida namoyon bo‘lib, kitobxonning hissiy xotirasi va assotsiativ tafakkurini faollashtiruvchi asosiy omil ekanligi isbotlandi._ 

**Kalit so‘zlar:** _psixolingvistika, badiiy matn, ona konsepti, assotsiatsiya, idrok, emotsionallik, nutq faoliyati, antropotsentrizm._ 

# **ABSTRACT** 

_This article presents a psycholinguistic analysis of O‘tkir Hoshimov’s novella “Dunyoning ishlari”. The study examines the perception of literary text by readers, the psychological impact of emotional and expressive units, the linguistic and psychological features of the mother concept, and associative thinking mechanisms. The influence of linguistic means on readers’ perception is interpreted through psycholinguistic theories. The findings reveal that the strong psychological effect of the novella is closely related to its emotional vocabulary, colloquial expressions, dialogic speech, and national-cultural concepts. The image of the mother functions as the conceptual core of the text and serves as the main factor activating emotional memory and associative thinking._ 

**Keywords:** _psycholinguistics, literary text, mother concept, association, perception, emotionality, speech activity, anthropocentrism._

Badiiy matnning psixolingvistik qimmati uning o‘quvchi tomonidan qanday qabul qilinishida namoyon bo‘ladi.
